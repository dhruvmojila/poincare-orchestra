# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributions from anyone. In issues, pull requests and discussions:

- Be factual and courteous. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Keep to the topic of the issue or pull request.
- Give credit, assume good faith, and accept that the maintainer makes the final call on scope and design.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks and spam are not accepted. The maintainer may edit, hide or delete such content and block repeat offenders. To report a problem, email dhruvmojila098@gmail.com; reports are handled in confidence.

This applies to every space of the project: the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any discussion that refers to them.
